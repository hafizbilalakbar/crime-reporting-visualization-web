Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about our platform

How do I report a crime?

Reporting a crime is simple. Just click on the "Report a Crime" button on our homepage or navigate to the Citizen Portal. Fill out the reporting form with as much detail as possible, including location, time, and any evidence you might have.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes, all personal information is kept strictly confidential. You can choose to report anonymously if you prefer. Only authorized personnel can access your contact information, and it will never be shared publicly.

How are reports verified?

All reports are reviewed by authorized administrators who check for completeness and accuracy. Reports may be cross-referenced with other sources and may require additional verification before being marked as confirmed and appearing on the public map.

Can I track the status of my report?

Yes, each report is assigned a unique reference number. You can use this number to check the status of your report at any time through our portal. You'll be able to see if it's pending review, verified, or requires additional information.
