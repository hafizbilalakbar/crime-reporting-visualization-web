Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I receive a response?

+

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call our support line for immediate assistance.

Is my information kept confidential?

+

Yes, all information submitted through our contact form is encrypted and kept strictly confidential. We adhere to stringent privacy standards and will never share your personal information with third parties without your consent.

How do I report a technical issue with the platform?

+

You can report technical issues through this contact form by selecting "Technical Support" in the subject dropdown. Please provide details about the issue you're experiencing, including any error messages and the steps to reproduce the problem.

Can I visit your office in person?

+

Yes, our office is open for visits during business hours (Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm). However, we recommend scheduling an appointment in advance to ensure that the appropriate team member is available to assist you.

How can I provide feedback about your service?

+

We value your feedback! You can share your thoughts and suggestions through the contact form by selecting "Feedback" in the subject dropdown. Your input helps us improve our platform and service quality.
