Understanding Our Crime Mapping System

CrimeWatch's interactive crime mapping system provides a visual representation of crime incidents in your community. By analyzing patterns and trends, citizens and law enforcement can work together to create safer neighborhoods.

Interactive Maps

Pan, zoom, and explore crime data with our user-friendly interface designed for all devices.

Heat Map Analysis

Identify crime hotspots with color-coded heat maps showing concentration of incidents.

Advanced Filtering

Filter by crime type, date range, and status to focus on specific areas of concern.

How to Use the Crime Map

Follow these steps to make the most of our crime mapping tool

1

Accessing the Map

Navigate to the "Crime Map" section from the main navigation menu. The map will load centered on your current location (if location access is granted) or a default central area.

2

Navigating the Map

Use the zoom controls to zoom in/out or drag to pan across the map. Click on markers to view details about specific incidents including type, date, time, and status.

3

Applying Filters

Use the filter controls to narrow down data by crime type (theft, assault, vandalism, etc.), time period (day, week, month, year), and status (verified, pending, resolved).

4

Switching Views

Toggle between pin view (individual markers) and heatmap view (density visualization) using the view toggle buttons. Heatmaps are excellent for identifying high-concentration areas.

5

Exporting Data

Use the export tools to download crime data as CSV, share a map screenshot, or generate a report of your selected area and filters for community meetings or personal reference.

Data Sources & Privacy

Understanding where our data comes from and how we protect privacy

Sources of Information

CrimeWatch maps display data from multiple verified sources:

  • Citizen-submitted reports (verified by our team)
  • Law enforcement agency data (where partnerships exist)
  • Public crime records and statistics
  • Community watch group reports (verified)

Privacy Protection

We take privacy seriously in our crime mapping:

  • Exact addresses are never displayed, only general vicinity
  • Personal information about victims or reporters is never shown
  • All data is anonymized before being added to the map
  • Verification process ensures sensitive details are removed

Data Updates

Understanding our update cycle:

  • New verified reports are added within 24 hours
  • Official data is updated weekly where available
  • Historical data is maintained for 12 months
  • Real-time updates available in select partner communities
