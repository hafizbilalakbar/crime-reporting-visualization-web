Safety Tips & Resources

Learn how to stay safe and protect your community

Personal Safety

  • Be aware of your surroundings, especially in high-risk areas
  • Avoid walking alone at night in poorly lit areas
  • Keep valuable items secure and out of sight
  • Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, move to a safe location
  • Have emergency contacts easily accessible on your phone

Home Security

  • Ensure all doors and windows have secure locks
  • Install adequate lighting around your property
  • Consider a security system or visible cameras
  • Don't leave spare keys in obvious locations
  • Join or form a neighborhood watch program
